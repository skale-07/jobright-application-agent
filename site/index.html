The grind is a loop.
Now the loop runs without you.

Job portals turned applying into a nightly ritual: the guilt, the forms, the maybe-reward, repeat. Dispatch keeps the loop — watch, arm, report, compound — and moves the ritual onto a machine. You keep the outcomes and the evenings.

01 · Watch

The dread used to start the night. Now the feed does.

The old starting gun was guilt — that 11pm feeling that you should be applying. Dispatch watches your feed instead, discovers new postings, and queues them itself. Opportunity pings you. Obligation doesn't.

what it replaces

what it replaces

what it replaces

One application's trip,
start to thank-you page.

Here's what actually happens to a single posting once Dispatch picks it up — including the annoying parts companies put in the way.

① found

Dispatch reads your JobRight feed and queues fresh postings. That's the beta discovery layer — more platforms are on the roadmap.

② the gates

"Apply now" is never the form. Redirects, "create an account", email verification codes — Dispatch navigates the maze, and fetches the code from your inbox itself. The one gate it won't fight: a CAPTCHA. That parks, for you.

③ the form

Your twelve fields, your resume, filled and then re-read from the page to prove it. No verified fill, no submit. Every submission ends with a receipt and a screenshot.

④ the referral

An application alone is a ticket in a pile. A referral is a person pulling yours out. After each verified submit, Dispatch drafts warm intros to alumni and engineers at that company — you review and hit send.

Why referrals get top billing: most applications die unread in a stack of hundreds. The reliable way out of the stack has always been a human on the inside who vouches for you. That's why the referral draft isn't an add-on here — it's the second half of every application. The submit gets you in the pile; the referral gets you out of it.

Set up once.
About thirty minutes, mostly waiting.

Dispatch is software you install, not an account you surrender your data to. Here's the whole onboarding, honestly.

you'll need

A laptop — macOS, Windows, or Linux — with Node 20+ and Chrome on it.

you'll need

A JobRight account — Premium, for unlimited applying and referral drafting during beta. It's the discovery layer Dispatch rides on until more platforms land.

you'll need

Your resume PDFs — a couple of versions, labeled by domain. Optional: Outlook for referral drafts, Gmail (read-only) so it can fetch verification codes.

1

Install it

One block, then coffee. It pulls its own browser.

npm install && npx playwright install chromium && npm run migrate

2

Feed it you — once

Your profile (the famous twelve fields), your resumes, any essay answers you've already sweated over. Everything lands in a private folder on your disk, nowhere else.

3

Log in like a human

You sign into JobRight in your own Chrome, once — Dispatch borrows that session instead of pretending to be you from scratch. Optional: connect Outlook (drafts) and read-only Gmail (verification codes).

4

Rehearse with the safety on

Start the console and run a dry pass: it discovers, plans every field, and touches nothing. You read exactly what it would have typed. This is the moment you learn to trust it — because it shows its work.

npm run console

5

Arm small, watch the first hour

First real session: short timer, low caps, you watching. After that first verified receipt lands with its screenshot — that's when you close the laptop and go get your evening.

Autonomy you arm.
Not autonomy you hope for.

The reason you can sleep while it runs isn't trust — it's architecture. Every claim below is enforced in code, not in a promise.

Bounded by construction

A session is a timer and two caps backed by an atomic counter. A crash can't reset it; disarming makes further submits structurally impossible.

Every gate still runs

Arming removes exactly one thing: the per-application "are you sure". Field verification, upload verification, page identity, the approved plan — all still gate every click.

Walls park, with evidence

CAPTCHA, logins, essay questions — anything that needs a human parks the application with a review item and its evidence. Nothing is force-clicked.

Your words stay yours

Essays and self-ID questions are never auto-answered. Referral outreach stops at drafts in your own mailbox — the software cannot send mail on your behalf.

Put one operator confidently in command of many applications — every submission deliberate, every state honest, every outcome accounted for. And the nights? Those are yours again.
