The grind is a loop.
Now the loop runs without you.

Job portals turned applying into a nightly ritual: the guilt, the forms, the maybe-reward, repeat. Dispatch keeps the loop — watch, arm, report, compound — and moves the ritual onto a machine. You keep the outcomes and the evenings.

01 · Watch

The dread used to start the night. Now the feed does.

The old starting gun was guilt — that 11pm feeling that you should be applying. Dispatch watches your feed instead, discovers new postings, and queues them itself. Opportunity pings you. Obligation doesn't.

what it replaces

Portal #47 wants the same twelve fields — retyped, because autofill never works there.

what it replaces

An essay wall at 11pm: "Why do you want to work here?" — 500 words, before the deadline.

what it replaces

The silence after submit. Did it even go through? No receipt, no record, no idea.

02 · Arm

Your whole job: one click, with hard bounds.

You set a timer and two caps, and press one button. Inside that window the machine discovers, fills, verifies, and submits. Outside it, nothing moves. Restart the console and it's disarmed. Always.

03 · Report

Wake up to a report, not a to-do list.

Every session ends differently — that's what makes opening the console interesting instead of dreadful. Verified submissions with receipts. Outreach drafts to real people, waiting in your mailbox. Walls parked with evidence attached. You read what happened; you don't do it.

the offer path

Verified submissions — screenshot, receipt, confirmation text. Never "probably went through".

the people path

Outreach drafts to alumni and engineers at each company — generated, validated, never sent without you.

the sanity path

A cleared queue. Anything that needed a human is parked with its evidence; everything else moved.

04 · Compound

Every answer you give, you never give again.

Each thing you feed Dispatch makes the next session need less of you — and overnight discovery reloads the queue, so the loop keeps turning on its own.

Your profile, once→ every form, every portal
An essay answer you wrote→ banked for every board that asks again
Your resumes, labeled by domain→ auto-attached per application
A persona for outreach→ drafts that sound like you
Every fill outcome→ selectors that learn which fields flake

Autonomy you arm.
Not autonomy you hope for.

The reason you can sleep while it runs isn't trust — it's architecture. Every claim below is enforced in code, not in a promise.

Bounded by construction

A session is a timer and two caps backed by an atomic counter. A crash can't reset it; disarming makes further submits structurally impossible.

Every gate still runs

Arming removes exactly one thing: the per-application "are you sure". Field verification, upload verification, page identity, the approved plan — all still gate every click.

Walls park, with evidence

CAPTCHA, logins, essay questions — anything that needs a human parks the application with a review item and its evidence. Nothing is force-clicked.

Your words stay yours

Essays and self-ID questions are never auto-answered. Outreach stops at drafts in your own mailbox — the codebase can't send mail, and CI proves it.

Put one operator confidently in command of many applications — every submission deliberate, every state honest, every outcome accounted for. And the nights? Those are yours again.
