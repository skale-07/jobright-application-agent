Fair questions.

Why do I need JobRight Premium too?

During beta, JobRight is the discovery layer — the feed Dispatch watches for new postings. Unlimited applying and referral drafting ride on that feed, and JobRight caps it on their free tier. We're building support for more discovery platforms; when those ship, the Premium requirement goes with them.

Is Dispatch open source?

No. Dispatch is a closed-source, paid product. What we promise instead of source code: it's local-first by design — your profile, resumes, answers, and history live on your disk, every submission produces evidence you can audit, and the software structurally cannot send email or submit without the bounds you set.

Does it send referral emails for me?

Never. It drafts them — into your own mailbox, addressed to real alumni and engineers at the company, written from your persona and validated before you ever see them. You read, edit, and hit send. Your name only ever goes out in your own hand.

What happens when it hits a CAPTCHA or a login wall?

It parks the application with the evidence attached and moves on to the next one. Nothing is force-clicked, nothing is guessed — the parked items are your short morning worklist.

Can it write my essays?

No — and that's a feature. Essay questions and self-ID questions are always yours. Dispatch banks the answers you write so you never write the same one twice, but the words are never invented for you.

Put one operator confidently in command of many applications — every submission deliberate, every state honest, every outcome accounted for. And the nights? Those are yours again.
